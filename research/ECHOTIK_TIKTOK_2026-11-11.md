## Free plan limits (what we could and could not get)

- Keyword search of the product library is blocked ("Free plan has reached the usage limit"). Workaround used: the search-box autocomplete (≈10 products with lifetime sales), then the product page.
- Autocomplete stopped answering after ~6 queries the same day (daily quota). Chin mask was not checked.
- Product page: lifetime totals + **only the last 30 days** of the daily chart. 90/180-day views, sales insights, comments, creator lists — paywalled.
- Dynamics below are therefore ◈: lifetime average per month (total ÷ months since listing) vs the last 30 days. Listing age is EchoTik's rounded "N months/years ago".
