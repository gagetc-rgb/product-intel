# Entry requirements, US: the four "go" products (2026-11-10)

Question: once a demand test passes, how hard is it to sell each product legally? What is already needed for the test (landing page + Meta ads), and what only before buying stock?

Scope: color-changing foundation, lash serum (prostaglandin-free), tallow balm, shower filter. Channel assumed: own store + Meta first, Amazon second.
Marks: ◆ primary source (regulator or platform) · ◈ secondary source or our reading · ◇ our inference, verify before relying on it.
This is a desk check, not legal advice. Costs are mostly not measured (see "Not checked").

## 1. Bottom line

| Product | Entry difficulty | The one thing that decides it |
|---|---|---|
| Color-changing foundation | Low | Ordinary cosmetic. No SPF claim, approved colour additives only |
| Tallow balm | Low to medium | Ordinary cosmetic, but no eczema / psoriasis / "heals" claims. Does it sell without them? |
| Shower filter | Medium to high | A showerhead is a regulated plumbing product: 2.5 gpm federal cap, and California bans sale unless the model is certified and listed. Health claims are not allowed |
| Lash serum | High | "Grows lashes" is a drug claim. The allowed wording ("appearance of fuller lashes") is weaker than what buyers search for. The small-business exemption probably does not apply |

None of the four needs a licence or pre-approval before a pre-order test. The ad wording is restricted from day one for all four, and that changes what the test measures.

## 3. Cosmetics law (foundation, tallow balm, lash serum)

Cosmetics need no FDA approval before sale. The 2022 law (MoCRA) added duties for the "responsible person": the company whose name is on the label. That would be us.

| Duty | Small business (under $1M average US cosmetic sales over 3 years) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Facility registration and product listing | Exempt, **unless** the product regularly touches the mucous membrane of the eye, is injected, is for internal use, or changes appearance for more than 24 hours. Renewal every 2 years; listing updated yearly. No fee found | ◆ [FDA guidance on registration and listing](https://www.fda.gov/media/170732/download), [FDA small business fact sheet](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/resources-industry-cosmetics/small-businesses-homemade-cosmetics-fact-sheet) |
| Label: a US address, US phone or website for adverse event reports | Not exempt | ◆ [FDA MoCRA page](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-laws-regulations/modernization-cosmetics-regulation-act-2022-mocra) |
| Serious adverse events reported to FDA within 15 business days | Not exempt | ◆ same |
| Safety substantiation records. No specific test is mandated; existing data may be used | Not exempt | ◆ same |
| Foreign factory: its facility registration must name a US agent | Applies to the factory. Ask the supplier for its FDA registration number | ◆ FDA guidance above |

The cosmetic / drug line is drawn by **intended use**, shown by the claims. A product sold to treat or prevent a condition, or to change how the body works, is a drug and needs approval or a monograph. ◆ [FDA: Is it a cosmetic, a drug, or both?](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-laws-regulations/it-cosmetic-drug-or-both-or-it-soap)

## 4. Per product

### 4.1 Color-changing foundation — low

- Colour additives must be FDA-approved for cosmetic use. Ask the factory for the full ingredient list with colour index numbers and check each against FDA's list. ◆ [FDA colour additives fact sheet](https://www.fda.gov/industry/color-additives/color-additives-and-cosmetics-fact-sheet)
- No SPF or sun-protection claim: makeup with one is also an over-the-counter drug. ◆ [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-laws-regulations/it-cosmetic-drug-or-both-or-it-soap)
- Small-business exemption applies (not an eye product, washes off within a day). ◈
- "Adapts to your skin tone" is an objective claim: keep shade-match evidence, expect returns on mismatch. ◇
- For the test: nothing beyond section 2.

### 4.2 Tallow balm — low to medium

- Cattle-derived material is restricted in cosmetics. Tallow is allowed if it has no more than 0.15% insoluble impurities, or is made without prohibited cattle materials. Get a supplier certificate for this. ◆ [FDA prohibited and restricted ingredients](https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-laws-regulations/prohibited-restricted-ingredients-cosmetics), [21 CFR 700.27](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/21/700.27)
- "For eczema", "psoriasis", "heals", "repairs the skin barrier" as treatment are drug claims. Allowed: moisturises, softens, for dry skin. ◆ FDA intended-use rule above
- Small-business exemption applies. ◈
- Open question for the test: tallow is often bought for skin conditions. The test must run with allowed wording only; if it does not convert without the treatment claims, the product is not sellable by us. ◇
- Made in the US by a contract maker, this avoids import duty and the US-agent question. ◇

### 4.3 Shower filter — medium to high

Not an FDA product. The difficulty is plumbing and claims.

- **Federal:** a showerhead may not exceed 2.5 gallons per minute at 80 psi. ◆ [DOE standards page](https://www1.eere.energy.gov/buildings/appliance_standards/product.aspx/productid/37)
- **California:** a showerhead must be certified to the Energy Commission and appear in its database (MAEDbS) before it is sold or offered for sale in the state; the cap there is 1.8 gpm. The Commission has settled cases against sellers of unlisted showerheads (sales in 2017-2021). ◆ [CEC settlement](https://efiling.energy.ca.gov/GetDocument.aspx?tn=259962&DocumentContentId=96166), [another](https://efiling.energy.ca.gov/getdocument.aspx?tn=249123)
- **Washington** also requires each showerhead model to be listed in MAEDbS. ◆ [WAC 194-24-155](https://lawfilesext.leg.wa.gov/law/WAC/WAC%20194%20%20TITLE/WAC%20194%20-%2024%20%20CHAPTER/WAC%20194%20-%2024%20-155.htm)
- Our margin calculation used a handheld showerhead with a built-in filter: that is a showerhead, so the rules apply. An inline filter (between the pipe and the customer's own showerhead) may fall outside them. ◇ Verify; it would also change the product and the price.
- Whether a private-label model can rely on the factory's listing, or needs its own, was not established. ◇
- **Claims:** "reduces chlorine" needs test evidence. The voluntary standard NSF/ANSI 177 covers free chlorine only and says nothing about chloramine or health. ◈ [trade press on NSF/ANSI 177](https://www.pmmag.com/articles/87589-web-exclusive-br-nsf-ansi-standard-177-a-level-playing-field-for-shower-filter-claims), [NSF listings](https://info.nsf.org/Certified/DWTU/listings.asp?Standard=177)
- "Stops hair loss", "clears acne", "helps eczema" are health claims with no evidence behind them. This is the leaders' main angle and it is the one we cannot copy. ◈
- "Kills bacteria" or any antimicrobial claim makes the filter a pesticide device: it must come from an EPA-registered establishment and carry the number. EPA fined one filter seller $150,000. ◆ [EPA pesticide devices guide](https://epa.gov/safepestcontrol/pesticide-devices-guide-consumers), [EPA device compliance advisory](https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2023-03/pesticidedevicecomplianceadvisory.pdf)
- For the test: nothing to register, but the page must avoid health and antimicrobial claims and should not take orders from California until the model is listed. ◇

### 4.4 Lash serum (prostaglandin-free) — high

- A claim that the product grows lashes makes it a drug. FDA seized one such product in 2007 and sent a warning letter in 2011 over growth claims and a prostaglandin analogue. ◈ [trade press on the 2011 letter](https://www.cosmeticsdesign.com/Article/2011/04/26/FDA-warns-eyelash-enhancer-makers-on-formulation-and-marketing-claims), [FDA 2007 seizure notice, archived](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/eot2008/20090115213426/http://www.fda.gov/consumer/updates/cosmeticeye111907.html)
- Amazon flags bimatoprost and isopropyl cloprostenate as prescription-only ingredients and removes listings. A prostaglandin-free formula avoids this, as our v2.2 verdict already requires. ◈ [seller forum](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/618a2240-96df-4b1d-84b8-18a2e8492f39)
- Allowed wording: "the appearance of longer, fuller lashes", "conditions". Not allowed: "grows", "regrows", "stimulates follicles". ◈
- The small-business exemption excludes products that regularly touch the mucous membrane of the eye. FDA's examples are eyeliner and lash adhesive; a serum applied along the lash line is close to both. Assume registration and listing are required. ◇
- Eye-area product: higher safety burden in practice (eye irritation testing) and higher insurance risk, though no specific test is mandated. ◈
- For the test: the ad must use the allowed wording. The demand we measured (search for "lash growth serum") is demand for the claim we cannot make.

## 5. What is needed when

| Stage | All four | Extra |
|---|---|---|
| Demand test (landing + Meta) | Allowed claims only; real shipping date if money is taken, or a no-payment waitlist | Shower filter: no California orders |
| Before buying stock | Company and insurance; label review; supplier documents | Cosmetics: ingredient and colour check, safety file, adverse-event contact on the label. Tallow: cattle-material certificate. Lash: FDA registration and listing. Shower: flow-rate test, California listing or a no-California policy, chlorine test report |
| Before Amazon | Category approval, liability insurance after the first $10K month | Lash: ingredient list ready for Amazon's checks |

## 6. Not checked in this pass

- Costs and lead times: safety assessment, stability and preservative testing, eye irritation testing, flow-rate testing, California certification, NSF/ANSI 177. No reliable public prices found; these need quotes.
- The current text of Amazon's Business Solutions Agreement section 9 and of its cosmetics policy (read only through secondary sources).
- Meta's live policy text on before-and-after for anti-aging products.
- California Proposition 65 warnings and lead-content rules for the shower filter.
- Whether MoCRA good-manufacturing-practice rules are final, and whether small businesses are exempt from them.
- Company set-up, sales tax and import-of-record questions for a non-US owner.
- The 2011 FDA warning letter in full text on fda.gov.

## 7. What this means for the method

- Entry difficulty differs a lot inside the "go" group, and METRICS.md D4 does not show it: it is one flag, not a cost or a gate.
- For lash serum and shower filter, the demand signals we measured are tied to claims a compliant seller cannot make. A product should be scored on demand for the **allowed** claim.
- Proposal, not applied: D4 becomes a short checklist per product (licences, claim limits, cost and time to first sale, what the test already needs), filled before a product can enter "go".
