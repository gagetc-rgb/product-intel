## Risks
- **Overlap with rosemary oil**, whose search interest is falling (×0.76): part of this shelf may shrink. ◈
- **Regulation**: "hair growth" is a drug claim in the US (minoxidil is the approved drug); cosmetic wording must be "thicker-looking". ◈
- **Three-quarters of the shelf is old brands.** ◆
- **Brand ages unverified.** ◈

## Verdict and what would change it
Challenger. It enters the top 5 if a compliance review finds a cheap cosmetic route, dated listings confirm the young brands, and its demand holds up separately from rosemary.
