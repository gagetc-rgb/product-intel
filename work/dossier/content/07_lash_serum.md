# Eyelash growth serum

**Verdict: challenger, not in the top 5 yet.** On Amazon numbers alone it would rank around #2-#3: a **$7.0 million-a-month** shelf where eight brands under 24 months sell 1,000+ a month. Two things hold it back: products that promise "growth" are regulated, which costs money and limits claims; and the brand ages are estimated from product codes, not dated, after the method already mistook RevitaLash for a new brand. ◆/◈

## The product, size and trend
A serum brushed on the lash line to make lashes look longer and fuller. Listings split into "prostaglandin-free" peptide serums (ForChics, EPAIS) and castor-oil ones (UpNature, unbranded). ◆
At least **213,450 purchases a month** on five Amazon pages (US, collected by hand), about **$7.0 million a month**; median price $26.98 (half between $17.99 and $44.00). Search for "lash serum" ×1.17 versus a year ago: **flat**, close to the growing line. ◆ count/price/trend, ◈ money.

## Ads
Meta, exact phrase "lash serum": 3,200 active; 1,100 (34%) 30+ days, 400 (12%) 90+, 90 (3%) 180+. 17 of 24 shops keep a 90+ day ad; oldest borboleta.com (725 days), moxielash.com (349). Brand Pages not counted yet. ◆

## Risks
- **Regulation.** In the US a product claiming to *grow* lashes is treated as a drug; prostaglandin-type ingredients carry side-effect warnings. A cosmetic route exists ("longer-looking"), but claims and ingredients must be reviewed. ◈ (our flag "COMPLIANCE": the route exists, it costs money)
- **Two-thirds of the shelf is old brands**, Grande at the top for years. ◆
- **Brand ages unverified**: "Date First Available" could not be collected. ◈

## Verdict and what would change it
Challenger. It enters the top 5 if (1) a compliance review shows a cheap cosmetic route, and (2) dated listings confirm the young brands are really young.
