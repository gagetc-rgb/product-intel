# Body oil for crepey skin

**Verdict: #2 of 20.** The shelf belongs to old drugstore brands, and demand is steady rather than growing. But one newcomer, **GODA**, went from zero to the top of the shelf in about 18 months: level with Palmer's as the #1 by revenue on Amazon, a large Meta presence run through creators, and 23,956 reviews on its own store. It is the clearest single proof among our 20 categories that a new brand can take a mature shelf. ◆/◈ (see below)

## 1. The product and the buyer

A body oil sold not for softness but against **crepey skin**: thin, wrinkled skin on arms, legs and chest that comes with age. GODA's listing reads "Silk Body Oil Women - Firming Anti Aging". The buyer is a woman 45+ who already uses a moisturizer and wants something that "firms". ◆ (listing titles)

Price: $19.47 median on Amazon (half between $12.99 and $28.25); GODA sells at $31.49, OSEA at $52. ◆

## 2. How big, and where it is going

**Size.** At least **196,800 purchases a month** on the first five Amazon search pages, about **$4.0 million a month** at listing prices. ◆ count, ◈ money.

**Trend.** Searches for "crepey skin" are flat: ×1.02 versus a year ago after correcting for the spring-2026 Google artifact. The yearly index has moved sideways for five years (50 → 58 → 43 → 58 → 69). This is a steady need, not a fad. ◆ (one pull of three)

◆ (brand age from ASINs, ±1-2 months)

Leaders by revenue (floor units × listing price): **GODA ~$0.63M/mo**, Palmer's ~$0.61M, OSEA ~$0.56M, Neutrogena ~$0.47M. GODA sells 20,000 a month from two listings, entered Amazon about 18 months ago, rated 4.3. ◆ units/price, ◈ revenue and age.

## 4. Can a newcomer get in

Hard, but proven. Three brands under 24 months sell 1,000+ a month; GODA is by far the largest. Unlike color foundation, there is almost no unbranded price war here (4,900 units): buyers choose brands. A newcomer has to look and feel like a brand from day one. ◈

## 5. What happens in ads

Meta Ad Library, US, active, exact phrase "crepey skin" (this phrase also catches cream ads): ◆

| Active | 30+ days | 90+ days | 180+ days |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11,000 | 2,900 (26%) | 1,100 (10%) | 84 (1%) |

A big ad market. 22 of 26 shops in our sample keep at least one ad 90+ days; the oldest are thehealthcarpenter.com (439 days) and try-turmskin.com (323 days, 19 ads in the sample). ◆

**How GODA advertises.** Only 140 active ads run from GODA's own Page, none older than 180 days. But **24 of 30 ads** that mention "Goda Silk" land on goda.co, and they come from creator pages ("Over 40 & Fabulous", "Natasha Knows Scents"). GODA's ad engine is creators, not its own Page. Its oldest live ad (262 days) is a creator post: "2 Drops That Will Change Your Life!" ◆

DRMTLGY, the other brand we counted: 1,200 active ads, 200 alive 90+ days, 36 alive 180+. ◆

## 6. Do the winners keep growing

Monthly review curve not measured yet. GODA's store shows **23,956 reviews** (Loox widget, 2026-11-07). Even at a high 10% review rate, that is about 240,000 orders; at a typical 3.7%, about 650,000. ◆ count, ◈ orders (review rates per Judge.me benchmark; varies 3-5× between stores).

## 7. What the winners say

Short emotional hooks from real-looking women 40+, often filmed by creators, promising visible change from a small amount ("2 drops"). ◆ (`work/ads/longrunning_v2.json`)

## 8. Risks and open questions

- **Flat demand.** Growth must come from taking share, not from a rising market. ◈
- **Creator dependence.** GODA's model needs a stream of creators; we see the ads, not the cost. ◈
- **The Meta phrase is wide** ("crepey skin" includes creams), so category ad counts overstate the oil-only market. ◆
- **Big old brands at low prices** (Palmer's $13.99, Aveeno $9.58) cap what a newcomer can charge on Amazon. ◆

## 9. Verdict and what would change it

**#2.** Not falling (×1.02); new brands take 26,600 units/mo; many shops with 90+ day ads. It would drop if search fell below ×0.8 or if GODA's share proved to be a one-off (watch its review curve).
