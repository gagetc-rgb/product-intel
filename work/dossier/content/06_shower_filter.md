# Filtered shower head

**Verdict: reserve (#6).** Demand is steady and the product is mid-priced, but on Amazon old brands hold two-thirds of the shelf and only one young brand passes 1,000 a month. The case for it is on Meta: **sproos** keeps 94 of its 130 ads alive for more than half a year, the most evergreen advertiser in our whole sample. ◆/◈

## The product, size and trend
A shower head with a built-in filter cartridge (against chlorine and hard water, sold for hair and skin), plus replacement cartridges: a repeat purchase. ◆ product, ◈ repeat.
At least **103,550 purchases a month** on five Amazon pages, about **$3.9 million a month**; median price $39.99 (half between $23.98 and $68.35). Search ×1.09 versus a year ago: **flat**. ◆ count/price/trend, ◈ money.

## Risks
