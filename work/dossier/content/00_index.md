# US launch: 20 categories, one method

We measured 20 product categories the same way and asked one question: **is there room for a new brand right now?**

Three independent signals, each read from the platform itself:

1. **Amazon:** how many people bought in the last month, and how much of it went to brands that are less than two years old. ◆
2. **Meta ads:** how many ads are running, and how many have been kept alive for 1, 3 and 6 months. Advertisers stop ads that lose money, so an ad that is still running after months is a sign it pays back. ◈
3. **Google searches:** is interest growing, flat or falling compared with a year ago. ◆

**The answer:** five categories pass every test.

| # | Category | In one line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Color-changing foundation | Young, fast-growing, already won by three new brands. |
| 2 | Body oil for crepey skin | Old shelf, but one newcomer (GODA) broke through. |
| 3 | Waterless scent diffuser | Premium, growing, won by store brands off Amazon. |
| 4 | Beef tallow balm | Most newcomer wins of all, but growth has stopped. |
| 5 | Persimmon soap | Fastest-growing search, small and fragile. |

Two serums (lash, hair) have strong Amazon numbers but carry regulatory cost; the filtered shower head is the reserve. Ten categories are weak and two are dropped because demand is falling.

**How sure are we?** Every number is marked: ◆ counted by a platform, ◈ an estimate or our reading, ◇ disputed. What we could not measure is said on each page.
