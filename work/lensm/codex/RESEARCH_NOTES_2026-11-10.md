### Sofyre contradictions and registration statement

[Product page](https://sofyre.com/products/mouse-repellent-pouches), 2026-11-10: automatic refills checkbox was checked at initial read; 30% discount shown. Product recommends replacing pouches every 90 days, while the [subscription policy](https://sofyre.com/policies/subscription-policy) says billing recurs every 30 days, cancellation at least three days before billing, and gives a $29.99-times-quantity formula. The relation between that generic policy formula and the particular product/renewal price remains unverified. The USD query did not give a reliable US-dollar display; no US price inferred. ◆

The page claims repellency, three-day results, long-term avoidance, 90-day duration, safety around children/pets/livestock and lists peppermint, cinnamon, cedarwood and castor oil. It describes peppermint concentration as 40% in one section and an 8% formula in another; the two descriptions are not reconciled. The footer says the product is not guaranteed to eliminate infestations. These are seller statements, not established effects or safety facts. No EPA or state registration statement was found in the readable product-page text, not proof of nonregistration. ◆

[EPA minimum-risk pesticide conditions](https://www.epa.gov/minimum-risk-pesticides/conditions-minimum-risk-pesticides) and [state regulation](https://www.epa.gov/minimum-risk-pesticides/state-regulation-minimum-risk-pesticides), read 2026-11-10: exemption from federal registration depends on all applicable conditions; states may separately require registration. Product eligibility, formulation, label, and state registrations have not been verified. ◆
