# Published US shipping reference, 2026-11-10

[USPS Notice 123](https://pe.usps.com/text/dmm300/Notice123.htm), effective 2026-10-04 and read 2026-11-10, lists Ground Advantage retail parcel prices of $8.40–$10.20 for packages up to 4 oz, and $10.05–$13.65 for packages over 8 oz through 1 lb, depending on zone ◆. This is postage only; packaging, pick-and-pack, taxes, special handling, dimensions and applicable surcharges are additional or unknown. ◆◈

These are conditional published rates, not product-specific fulfillment costs: the actual packed weight, dimensions, origin and destination of the specified leader products have not been verified. Do not treat the weights above as actual product weights. ◈
