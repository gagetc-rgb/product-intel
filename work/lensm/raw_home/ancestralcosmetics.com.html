100.000+ Skin Transformations

Irish grass-fed tallow makes all the difference.

Our skincare starts long before it reaches your hands.

It begins on Irish land, under soft rain and endless green pasture.

Ireland’s mild climate and rich soil create one of the most unique environments in the world for raising healthy, grass fed animals.

Cows here graze outside for most of the year, moving through fields that stay green even in winter. This slow, natural way of living produces tallow that is richer, softer and more nourishing than anything created in intensive farming systems.

  • Fresh suet only.

    Every week local farmers deliver fresh fat to our facility in Enniscorthy, in Ireland. Not frozen, not oxidised and never stored for months. Fresh, clean and full of natural nutrients.

  • We melt it.

    We render it ourselves with low heat and patience, the same way our families did for generations. This is hard, manual labour. No other brand is crazy enough to go through this pain.

  • Hand made.

    Every jar is hand crafted in Ireland. Because skincare should not be fake, watered down or mass produced. It should be real, honest, nourishing. Made with ingredients so clean you can eat them.
