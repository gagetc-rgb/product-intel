Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Everstride socks and other brands?

Simply put... we've created compression socks you will actually want to wear!

Unlike other brands that are either too tight, difficult to put on, or uncomfortable, our socks are designed for easy wear while offering comfortable and effective relief.

What is Everstride's 60-Day Fit Guarantee?

While our sizing is as accurate as possible, sometimes the fit just isn't right.

That's why we have our 60-Day Fit Guarantee.

Try our socks on, wash them and if you're not 100% satisfied with the fit within 60 days of receiving your order, simply email us with your order number and the size you need, and we'll send you the new size free of charge — no need to return the original product.

info@everstridesocks.com

Does the foot size increase with larger sock sizes?

Yes, as the sizes increase, the foot bed gets larger too. We understand that people with wider calves often have larger feet as well, so this ensures the fit stays comfortable and supportive for all sizes.

How long can I wear the socks for?

You can wear our compression socks throughout the whole day, typically for 8-12 hours.

They’re designed for extended use, providing support and comfort whether you’re on your feet, sitting for long periods, or staying active.

It is recommended to take them off before going to bed unless advised otherwise.

How tight are Everstride socks and what kind of pressure will I experience?

Our compression socks use a graduated compression of 15-20mmHg, offering the perfect balance of support and relief without feeling too tight.

This gentle, gradual compression ensures a consistent support from your ankle to your calf, making them comfortable enough for all-day wear while still effectively reducing swelling and discomfort.

How do I wash Everstride socks?

Our socks are durable and can be washed repeatedly without losing their shape or effectiveness.

- Water temperature below 40℃ or 105℉
- Turn the socks inside out
- Do not use bleach
- Do not tumble dry
