Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

We offer FREE and Fast Insured Shipping for all orders above $40.Order processing takes 1-3 business days before shipment. Once your item/s is dispatched, the estimated delivery time is:
- USA: 2-5 business days
-Canada: 2-7 business days
Note: Orders are shipped across different warehouse located in Nevada and Pennsylvania.

Do you offer free returns?

We have an iron-clad, risk-free 30-day guarantee that begins only when you receive the product! If you don't have a positive experience for any reason, contact us any time and we will help you out. 

How can I track my order?

You'll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships, allowing you to monitor delivery progress.

Are your products safe for sensitive skin?

Yes! All our fragrances are dermatologist-tested, alcohol-free, and formulated with gentle, skin-friendly ingredients.

How long do fragrances last on the skin?

Our oil-based formulas provide exceptional longevity, typically lasting 8-12 hours with beautiful scent evolution throughout the day.

What makes your fragrances different?

Our fragrances are pheromone-infused and oil-based, creating longer-lasting, more intimate scents that work with your natural body chemistry.
