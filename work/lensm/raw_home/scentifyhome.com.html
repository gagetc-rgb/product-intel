    Got Questions? We've Got Answers!

    Is it easy to set up?
    Simply twist the bottle of fragrance oil into the diffuser and plug it into any outlet.
    Does the diffuser include oil?
    The oils are sold separately. The diffuser will come with an empty bottle if no oils are purchased.
    How large of an area does it cover?
    • Plug-In Diffuser V2: ideal for bedrooms, bathrooms, and smaller spaces — up to 800 sq. ft.
    • Tower Diffuser: great for larger bedrooms, offices, and living rooms — up to 1,000 sq. ft.
    • Lux Diffuser: designed for open-concept spaces and larger areas — up to 1,500 sq. ft.

    Coverage may vary based on room layout, airflow, and intensity settings.

    How often should I refill the bottle?
    Depending on how you set the diffuser, each bottle typically lasts 4-8 weeks.
    Are the oils safe?

    Scentify oils are crafted from a blend of natural and synthetic ingredients and are manufactured in the U.S. in compliance with IFRA standards. Our oils are free from parabens and phthalates, are vegan and non-GMO, and are designed for safe ambient fragrance use when used as directed. Keep out of reach of children and pets.
