Common Questions

How long until I notice a difference?

Most customers notice a difference within 3–7 days. For best results, use the Persimmon Soap and Body Wash together in your daily shower routine.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Yes. Our formulas are developed for mature skin and are free from harsh detergents and artificial fragrances that commonly irritate sensitive skin.

Does it mask odor or eliminate it?

Mirai products are designed to neutralize odor-causing compounds rather than simply covering them with fragrance.

What's in it? Is it really all-natural?

We use Japanese botanical ingredients including persimmon extract, plus gentle plant-based cleansers. See each product page for the full ingredient list.

How is this different from regular deodorant?

Unlike typical deodorants that mask odor on one area, Mirai's body care addresses odor across the skin with persimmon-powered cleansing for head-to-toe freshness.
