    Happy Cats Get The Ka-Zoomies!

    Catnip plays an important role in keeping cats mentally and physically stimulated. A stimulated cat is a happy cat!

    • Triggers Natural Instincts — Catnip activates your cat's hunting and play drive, giving them a healthy outlet for their wild side.
    • Reduces Stress & Anxiety — A good catnip session melts away tension and leaves cats calm, content, and purring.
    • Encourages Exercise — Rolling, jumping, and chasing keeps cats active and helps maintain a healthy weight.
    • 100% Natural — No additives, no fillers. Just pure, potent catnip the way nature intended.

    1.
    Cat Sniffs The Catnip

    The magic starts with the nose. When cats smell nepetalactone — the active compound in catnip — it binds to receptors in their nasal tissue.

    2.
    The Brain Goes Wild

    Nepetalactone mimics feline pheromones and triggers a euphoric response in the brain, similar to a natural high.

    3.
    The Ka-Zoomies Begin

    Rolling, rubbing, flipping, vocalizing — your cat is in pure bliss. This active phase typically lasts 5–10 minutes.

    4.
    Ready To Go Again

    After about 30 minutes the receptors reset and your cat is ready for another round. Safe, natural, and endlessly entertaining.

    1.
    Offer The Catnip

    Sprinkle pinch of catnip in a refillable toy, on a scratching post, or directly on the floor. A little goes a long way.

    2.
    Watch The Magic Happen

    Within seconds your cat will roll, rub, flip, and zoom. The active compound nepetalactone triggers an instant euphoric response.

    3.
    Enjoy The Afterglow

    After 5–10 minutes the effect naturally fades, leaving your cat relaxed and satisfied.
