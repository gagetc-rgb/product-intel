HOW IT WORKS

Science-Backed Protection in 3 Simple Steps

Fresh Cab® doesn't just cover up the problem with a strong smell. It disrupts the way pests communicate and navigate - making treated spaces unreadable and inhospitable to pests.

Step 1

Disrupt Pests

Pests rely on chemical scent trails to find food, navigate safe paths, and communicate with others. Our formula scrambles these signals, making the area functionally invisible to unwanted visitors.

Step 2

Slow-Release Delivery

Each pouch contains oils proven to deter pests embedded in a proprietary base of corn cob and plant fibers. This releases the active ingredient gradually over 30 days, no daily reapplication needed.

Step 3

Pests Leave, You Stay

Without scent trails, pests perceive the treated area as unsafe and avoid it entirely. No harsh chemicals, no dead pests in your walls, no toxic residue. Take back your space.
