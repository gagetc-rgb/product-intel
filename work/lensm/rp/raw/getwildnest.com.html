    Straight answers.

    What size do I order?

    They run a touch generous and the cuff stretches wide, so order your normal shoe size. Between sizes, or a high instep? Size up. If the fit is off, send them back.

    Is there any wool in them?

    Not a thread. No wool means no itch, no rash, and nothing that shrinks or felts in the machine. It is a performance knit that moves sweat off your skin instead of holding it.

    Will they cook my feet in summer?

    That is what the vent panels are for. Mesh over the top of the foot, no cotton anywhere — cotton is what soaks through and stews by lunch.

    How do I wash them?

    Machine wash cold, tumble dry low, straight in with your work clothes. No hand-washing, no hang-drying, no special soap.

    How long does delivery take?

    Orders leave us within one to three business days with a tracking number. Shipping is calculated at checkout.
