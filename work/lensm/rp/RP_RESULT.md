## Could not be read, and why
- `meta_ages.py` test run on "tallow balm": the active count came back None; re-read once after 60 s: 2,500 (`recount_rp_test_active.json`). The test sample itself was sane (56 ads, all dated, 39 with a domain). No login wall or check appeared at any time. ◆
- "rodent repellent pouches": active count None on the first run; re-read once after 60 s: 8,700 (`recount_rp_rodent_1_active.json`). The JSON file itself still holds None (the script cannot overwrite). ◆
- "pet urine odor remover" and "cat dental sticks": Meta returned 0 active ads and 0 sample, all counts 0 (the page said no match; not None). Not replaced, as instructed. ◆
- "insoles for standing all day": 1 ad only (PowerStep, no domain). ◆
- vivaia.com: HTTP 403 on the first request, recorded as blocked, no retry (an off-target shoe store). ◆
- Prices: where the home page showed only a `$0.00` cart value (petmizer.com, lumibellabeauty.com, getwildnest.com) the price is "not read". Prices that appear on a page are in USD in every case read (page currency tags USD); none were converted. For grandpagus.com and bugmd.com the price is a page price not matched to the product.
- RDAP: no answer (HTTP 404) for medicube.us and cosyisland.co; both are listed "unknown" and classed by Wayback only.
- Wayback "none" (no capture) for many young domains (e.g. petmizer.com, beautywithyuri.com, movaracare.com); for these the age class rests on the RDAP date alone.
- Ads that show no landing domain (14 to 35 per phrase) could not be assigned to a domain store; they are grouped by page name in the CSV (`page:` rows) and were not fetched.
- The on-target share by domain name alone is mostly "unclear" for beauty, cat and rodent products (brand names do not say what is sold). Step C store pages are the better reading.
- Step C covered only domains with an oldest sampled ad of 90+ or under 30 days; stores between 30 and 89 days (for example officialyuri.com, kiierr.com, sofyre.com 46 ads, nestoff.com, heycomfora.com) were not checked.
- Step D counts of 0 at 30+ for quiabeauty.com, cevorahealth.com, valleskin.com, lapcosusa.com, lumibellabeauty.com and bugmd.com were returned by Meta as 0, not None, but I could not re-check them independently.

## Anything that looked wrong
- Meta's own counts vary a lot by phrase for the same product: collagen mask 17 / 21 / 1,800 active; insoles 33 / 1 / 430; silvervine 300 / 30 / 0.
- beautywithyuri.com and yurikoreanbeauty.com serve the same "Official YURI" store (same title, same prices) and officialyuri.com is a third domain of it by name; they must not be counted as separate stores. beautywithyuri.com shows 1 active ad, yurikoreanbeauty.com 160.
- Step D counts are Meta's count for the domain used as a text phrase (the earlier pass showed that this finds ads that mention the domain in the text; it may include ads of other pages).
- Superfeet: 380 ads at 90+ but 0 at 180+ although the domain exists since 1996; shows how little the 180+ count says about the store's real age.
- Stepprs: first Wayback capture (2009) is older than the RDAP registration (2024-01-31); the domain may have been re-registered. Not young by either date as the rule is written.
- The sheets in `stores_<product>.csv` are the step A/B output; `stores_<product>_full.csv` adds steps C and D.
- One background job (rodent phrases 2 and 3 plus later) stopped silently after its first line; I re-ran those two phrases by hand, in order, with 30 s between runs.
