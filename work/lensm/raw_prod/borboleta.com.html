    How To Use

    Lash Serum

    • One application per night is all it takes to achieve visible results in as little as 4 weeks, and full results in 8 weeks.
    • For best results, start by removing your makeup and thoroughly cleansing your face and lash line.
    • On a clean and dry face/eye area, carefully & slowly apply in one single stroke starting from the inner eye area to the outer eye, close to base of the top lash line. Avoid direct contact to the eye. Do not apply to lower lash line area. Allow serum to dry down before moving on to your next routine.

    Brow Serum

    • As part of your evening routine, use the in-bottle applicator to dip once and apply serum to cleansed, dried eyebrows. Brush serum gently yet firmly using the applicator, coating from near the base to the tips of the brow hairs, ensuring the brush stays within the contours of the eyebrow area.
    • Do not exceed one application per eyebrow each day. Wipe away any excess product and allow it to dry for 90 seconds before continuing with routine. For best results use nightly as directed. Brow Serum should only ever be used on eyebrows.

    Why we're obsessed

    No matter your go-to look, whether you like a naked eye or extensions, Lash Serum creates the perfect foundation of strong, healthy looking lashes—looking as long and full as possible. And with the same formula, our Brow Serum gives you a healthier, fuller looking brow line.
