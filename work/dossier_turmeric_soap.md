# Dossier: turmeric soap for melanin-rich skin (2026-11-07)

All data collected on 2026-11-07 unless dated. ◆ = confirmed from a primary page; ◈ = likely, derived; ◇ = uncertain.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **The need is real and large.** Amazon's kojic/turmeric soap leader sells ~50K units a month at $13-15, and "turmeric soap" search grew ~5× in 2021-24 and then held.
2. **The premium "melanin-rich" DTC lane is won by one brand so far.** Earthly Derm grows ~10× in 8 months on every independent signal: reviews, traffic, brand search and ads. The others are flat (Honey Glow), fading (Kaoli, Kaavi) or just ramping (Stem & Soul).
3. **Entry is getting crowded right now.** About 220 of the 320 active "turmeric soap" ads in the US started in the last ~2.5 weeks.
4. **To win we need what Earthly Derm has:**
   - a better-than-generic formula (a syndet, face-safe bar);
   - several angles: dark spots, razor bumps for men, acne;
   - a subscription;
   - a review engine;
   - a US warehouse.
   We should not copy its drug-type claims or its persona advertorial.
5. **Open:**
   - ad history via BigSpy (needs the owner's login);
   - Keepa for Amazon;
   - landed cost from a cosmetic (not handmade) manufacturer.

## 6. What Earthly Derm does (offer and funnel)

- One hero SKU in three listings with different angles:
  - "Fade & Glow" for dark spots and acne marks;
  - "Glow / Bump Bar" for razor bumps, aimed at men;
  - an acne angle.
- Pricing and terms:
  - $42 / $49 / $69 bundles;
  - a subscription ("ships every 30 days");
  - a 60-day money-back guarantee;
  - "1 bar = 30 days".
- Funnel:
  - an advertorial page (/pages/earthlyderm-adv1) written as a first-person story;
  - a quiz (/pages/quiz-result);
  - a "100,000+ members" claim.
- Ships from an Indiana warehouse in 2-4 days.
- Review machine: Loox photo reviews (~900/mo), plus a Trustpilot invitation flow (~20/week; some reviews praise named CS agents).

### Claims risk (do NOT copy)
- "Regulating melanin production", "fades dark spots at the source", "Deep-Penetration Technology™", "acne stops at the source", "clears bacteria" are drug-type claims under FDA rules (◈).
- The first-person advertorial may count as a fake testimonial (FTC 16 CFR 465), unless it is a real customer.
- Our version must stay cosmetic: "cleanses, evens the look of tone, brighter-looking skin".

## 7. Cost side (rough)

- Alibaba private-label turmeric + kojic bars: ◆ list prices $0.28-1.00 per 100 g bar, MOQ 5-1,000.
  - These are mostly cold-process "handmade" bars.
  - Earthly Derm claims a syndet bar at pH 5. That is a different, better-for-face formula and needs a cosmetic manufacturer, not a soap generic.
- China duty on soap (HTS 3401.11.50): 37.5% (from earlier work). No de minimis, so ship to a US warehouse.
- ◈ Landed cost for a branded box: ~$1.5-3 per bar, against a $42-69 basket at Earthly Derm. Gross margin is not the problem; CAC and claims are.
- Alternative: a US contract manufacturer (◇ quotes not collected). It avoids the 37.5% duty and gives a "Made in USA" story.
