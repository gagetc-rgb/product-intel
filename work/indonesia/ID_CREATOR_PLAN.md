## 3. Market facts that shape the choice

- ◆ TikTok Shop ID beauty & personal care, July 2026: 113M units, ~IDR 10.35T GMV/month, +34% YoY; 3.8M creators promote it (FastMoss via dami.co.id). Biggest and most crowded.
- ◇ Magpie IQ (different panel): TikTok Shop beauty GMV fell ~10% Jan-May 2026 while Shopee beauty grew 14%. Panels disagree; treat beauty growth as uncertain.
- ◆ Fastest-growing category: Automotive & Motorcycle, +159% YoY GMV, 37.4M units/month (FastMoss, July 2026).
- ◆ Home care is the fastest FMCG e-commerce category in H1 2026, +78% YoY (Compas via Shopdora).
- ◆ Fragrance: Indonesia is the region's largest fragrance market (~$401M on one marketplace, 12 months to Mar 2026; Moojing). FastMoss: fragrances +28% MoM in July 2026.
- ◆ Hair care: +24% YoY in ID (Moojing); hair tonics top the Involve Asia conversion list.
- ◆ Winning formats (Involve Asia, Kalodata): price under IDR 100-200K, demo / before-after, BPOM proof, replenishable.
- ◆ Repeat tactic that worked (Cube): "hero bundle" — hero SKU + 20%+ bundle discount, own videos and lives, then 30+ creators.

## 4. Rules that limit the test

- ◆ Cosmetics need a BPOM notification before any listing: 14-60 days, IDR 500K (ASEAN-made) or 1.5M (non-ASEAN) per SKU, filed by an Indonesian company.
- ◆ Halal for cosmetics and household goods: deadline moved to 17 Oct 2028 by PP 40/2026 (BPJPH, 2026-10-24). Articles saying "Oct 2026" are outdated.
- ◆ TikTok Shop ID pre-order opened Nov 2026: 3-15 days to ship, +3% fee, store rating >= 3, seller-fault cancellation <= 7% (tukemarketing).
- ◆ Seller-fault cancellations cut the store's AHR score by up to 10 points (meagency, 2026 rules).
- ◈ Indonesian consumer law (UU 8/1999, art. 16) bars failing to deliver orders as promised. A fake "sold out" after taking money is a legal and account risk.
- ◆ Fees: platform commission 6.97-8% + dynamic commission 3-8% + IDR 1,250 per order; affiliate commission on top (8-12% typical for beauty, ◈).

## 5. Recommendation

### Step 0 — free demand test with zero stock (TikTok Shop affiliate)
Creators promote existing best-sellers in 5 candidate categories via TikTok Shop affiliate links. They earn commission, we pay nothing,
and we see per creator: views, clicks, orders. Output: orders per 1,000 views by creator x category. This is the honest version of
"test before stock", and it also shows which of the 98 creators actually sell.

Candidate categories to put in front of the creators (one product each, a top seller from Kalodata/FastMoss):
1. Motorcycle care consumables (chain lube, helmet cleaner/deodoriser spray, motor shampoo, quick coating) — fastest-growing category, consumable, fits male creators. CORRECTION 2026-11-08: no BPOM, but motor shampoo and helmet fresheners need a Kemenkes PKRT permit (see section 7); chain lube may need lubricant registration (◇ unchecked).
2. Home cleaning consumables (stain-remover powder, bathroom/kitchen cleaner, laundry scent booster) — fastest FMCG growth, demo-friendly, replenishable. ◈ May need a Kemenkes PKRT permit; check.
3. Local fragrance (body mist / EDP 30-50 ml) — large market, collection buying. BPOM needed.
4. Hair care (anti-hairfall tonic / serum) — top conversions, high repeat. BPOM; avoid drug claims.
5. Viral snack / drink sachet — F&B is TikTok's #3 category, highest repeat. Heaviest rules (BPOM MD or PIRT, halal for food already in force). Test last.

### Step 1 — own SKU in the winning category
- Local contract maker (maklon) or local wholesale stock, so orders ship in 1-2 days; first batch 100-300 units.
- Or pre-order (3-15 days) once the store qualifies. No fake "sold out".
- Build in repeat from day 1: refill pack, 2-3 pack bundle, store-follower voucher, weekly live.

### Step 2 — scale
Kill/keep thresholds set before launch from margin (price - COGS - ~25-30% platform+affiliate take - shipping). Track repeat at 30/60/90 days.

### Permit tiers for our own version
- ◆ Kemenkes PKRT permit (household health supplies): air fresheners of every form (spray, gel, hanging, car, helmet, reed diffuser,
  candles, automatic), cleaners, detergent, fabric perfume, toilet fresheners, car shampoo, tissues and cotton, insect repellents.
  Needs a PT/CV; state fee about IDR 500K-1.5M per product; valid 5 years. ◇ Consultants claim 10 working days.
- ◆ BPOM notification: anything on the body (cosmetics, incl. toothpaste, body mist, hair tonic). 14-60 days.
- ◈ No permit found: plain hardware and non-chemical refills (filter cartridges, paper liners, mop pads). Verify per product.

### Smoking
- ◆ Cigarettes and vapes: prohibited on TikTok Shop; PP 28/2024 bans online sale without age checks and bans producers/importers/distributors
  from promoting them on social media. Shopee also bans filters and rolling papers.
- ◇ TikTok Shop rules on smoking accessories (filters, ashtrays) not confirmed.
- Legal way to reach smokers: smell removal (car, room, clothes, helmet) and oral care for smokers.

### How to read repeat demand from affiliate tests
- Affiliates do not see who re-buys. ◈ Free proxy: on the seller's shop, compare "terjual" (sold) counts of the refill listing vs the device
  listing, and watch both weekly. A refill count that keeps pace with the device count means people come back.
- Per creator x product: orders per 1,000 views.
- Clone only if both are good; thresholds to be set from our margin before the test (not yet set).
