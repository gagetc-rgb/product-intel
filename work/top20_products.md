# Top-20 product hypotheses for a US launch (from Daily Ecommerce 2026)

Date: 2026-11-06. Source: 138 in-scope long videos (briefs by NotebookLM), plus quick US web checks.
Scores are analyst judgment on a 1-5 scale, not measured data. Revenue figures in the videos are the author's estimates and were NOT used as a criterion.

## Criteria and weights

- **D** (20%): US demand signal (US-targeted competitor ads, ad longevity, US market presence)
- **C** (15%): Competition / room to differentiate (5 = open niche or clear angle)
- **R** (20%): Regulatory ease in US (5 = none; FDA/CPSC/FCC/EPA/claims risk lowers)
- **O** (10%): Ops & logistics (light, unbreakable, no battery/liquids, few sizes, low returns)
- **E** (15%): Unit economics (price >= $35, markup >= 3x, bundles, repeat purchase)
- **A** (15%): AI-creative reproducibility (visual demo, long-running ads to model)
- **T** (5%): Timing fit for a launch Dec 2026 - Mar 2027

Hard exclusions before scoring: ingestibles; drug or disease claims (hair/lash growth serums, neuropathy creams, berberine patches, smoking-cessation inhalers, dark-spot 'treatment'); medical devices needing FDA clearance for the advertised use (LED masks, red-light hearing devices, EMS neck devices, baby nebulizer, microneedling); infant sleep positioner (Safe Sleep for Babies Act); EPA-regulated rodent repellent; pseudoscience wellness (grounding sheets, frequency generators, 'virility' bracelets); saturated electronics (CarPlay adapters, mini projectors, steam cleaners); digital products and methodology-only videos; summer-only items (dog car fan, tanning balm).
