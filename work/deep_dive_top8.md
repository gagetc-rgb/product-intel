# Deep dive: top-8 products — creatives, funnels, live US ad check

Date: 2026-11-06. Sources: (1) full transcripts of the 8 Daily Ecommerce videos (same indexed text as in NotebookLM; on-screen visuals are not in the text); (2) live Meta Ad Library, filter Country = United States, Active ads, sorted by impressions, checked by us today.

Ad counts are Ad Library search hits ("~N results"), not exact ad counts; keyword search on a domain can also catch ads that only mention it. Revenue figures are the YouTube author's estimates (Winning Hunter / his own "revenue estimator" from active-ad counts) and are **not verified**.

Hooks below are paraphrased, not quoted.

## Cross-cutting findings

1. **Same 3-step system everywhere:** a cold ad (long story video or a short demo), then an advertorial ("5/7/10 reasons why"), then a long product page with a bundle picker, then retargeting with statics ("we made too much", "buy 2 get 2", "sale ends"). Short-demo products (ski sleeve, bracelet) skip the advertorial.
2. **Long AI story ads aimed at 50+ audiences.** The author says it outright: AI actors are less likely to be called out by older viewers. Menvault (3:26), Swarva (2 min) and Luhxe (2 min) all use 1.5-3.5 min first-person AI stories.
3. **Fake-persona pages are common** (e.g. "Over 60 & Thriving", a "flight attendant for 23 years", "Dr. Margaret Ellis", "co-founder Monica" posting from a personal page). Do **not** copy this. The FTC rule on consumer reviews and testimonials (16 CFR Part 465, in force since Oct 2024) bans fake testimonials, including ones attributed to people who do not exist. Our versions must be clearly brand-voiced, a labelled dramatization, or real customers.
4. **Fake scarcity** ("we made too much", "this is the end", "we only do this once a year") recurs. It is an FTC deception risk if untrue. Use real promotions (BFCM, real stock limits).
5. **Ad longevity is the best free signal.** Several top-impression ads have run 6-12 months (SkiGuards since 6 Nov 2025, Skult since Sep 2025, Menvault since Dec 2025).

## AD pass 1 (devil's advocate on this shortlist), 2026-11-06

Attacks:
1. **The "US demand" score rests on ad counts, the same proxy we criticise the author for.** Ad Library hits are inflated by duplicates (SkiGuards: 23 of the 24 top ads share one caption). Keyword search also catches other advertisers (the Luhxe "~490" includes an email-agency page). Ads shown under the US filter do not tell us what share of spend goes to the US: SkiGuards' top ad reached mostly Germany/Denmark.
2. **Selection bias plus guru exposure.** Every product was picked by a course seller and shown to thousands of viewers who clone it. The persimmon-soap flood (5+ brands with the same story) is what happens next. The ski video is 3 days old, so clones will hit that niche this season.
3. **No marketplace data used.** The plan in HANDOFF calls for Keepa/BSR, TikTok Shop GMV and Google Trends. None was checked. There is no landed cost, tariff rate or margin, so the "economics" score is a guess.
4. **Compression tank ranked #3 because its ads are AI, which is a production criterion, not demand.** Its US ads only started 17 Sep 2026: no US longevity, against our own "longevity is the best signal" rule. Apparel with 7 sizes means returns. "Man boobs" hooks may break Meta's personal-attributes policy.
5. **Leak-proof boxers were demoted by score arithmetic despite the strongest evidence:** ~100% US traffic, US-specific post-prostatectomy targeting, ads live since Dec 2025, $149.95 six-pack, repeat purchase.
6. **Ski sleeve timing.** Sample, bulk order, freight and customs leave in-US stock in mid-December at best, for a Dec-Feb peak. Leftover stock sits for 9 months. The US market share of SkiGuards is unknown; cheap Amazon gel pads set a price anchor.
7. **Bracelet in Q4** means launching a new pixel into peak BFCM/Christmas CPMs in the most crowded Meta category. "Never tarnishes" lives or dies on supplier quality; tarnish leads to refunds and chargebacks.
8. **"Recreate competitor creatives"** carries copyright and trade-dress risk if done literally (the author's mirror tooling).

What survives / changes:
- **Leak-proof boxers move back into the top 3**, pending an FDA check (incontinence garment classification and registration duties) and a sample absorbency test.
- **Ski sleeve: conditional.** Go only if a supplier can land US stock by ~10 Dec and US search demand is confirmed; otherwise prepare for the 2027 season (pre-season ads from July, as SkiGuards did).
- **Bracelet: keep, but aim at Valentine's** (assets in Dec, launch mid-Jan), not BFCM.
- **Compression tank: down to reserve** until there is 60+ days of US ad longevity and a size/returns plan.
- **Process fix:** before any purchase, run the marketplace data check (Amazon BSR/Keepa, Google Trends, TikTok Shop) and a margin plus break-even CAC model on the 3. Creatives: copy structure and hooks, not footage or layouts.
