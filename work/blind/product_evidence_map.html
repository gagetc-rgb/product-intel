Step 1 · How do we know a product sells?

Three witnesses, each sees a different part of the market

Nobody publishes a competitor's sales. So we ask three sources that leak sales indirectly. A product counts as selling if at least one witness sees strong sales. The more witnesses agree, the more we trust it.

Witness 1 · Amazon

"10K+ bought in past month"

  • Amazon prints this under each listing. We add it up for the top 20 results.
  • Strong if ≥ 30,000 units a month.
  • Blind to: products sold only on own websites.
Witness 2 · TikTok Shop

"819K sold"

  • Every TikTok Shop product shows a lifetime sales counter. We take the best seller in the category.
  • Strong if ≥ 50,000 sold.
  • Blind to: the date. A low number can be a bad page, so low = "unknown".
Witness 3 · Meta ads

Ads running 6+ months

  • Nobody pays for an ad for half a year if it loses money.
  • Counted as confirmation only.
  • Blind to: TikTok and Amazon sellers. Noisy: the search also catches salons and clinics.
Step 2 · The filter

Four gates, same for every product

A product must pass all four. The gates were fixed before the final ranking was read.

GateQuestionRule
1 DemandDoes anyone buy this at scale?Amazon ≥ 30K units/month or TikTok best seller ≥ 50K sold
2 RoomCan a newcomer sell, or does one brand own it?At least 5 different Amazon listings each selling 1K+/month
3 ValueIs one customer worth paying for?Bought again (consumable, refill) or price ≥ $40
4 LegalCan it be sold honestly?Not banned, core promise not false

Step 5 · Devil's advocate

We attacked our own numbers. Here is what broke.

The previous pass put turmeric soap at #60 of 94. That came from one bad TikTok page. A second page for the same category showed the real leader. The same happened with the exfoliating towel.

TikTok best seller: first page vs second page

Lesson: a single low reading means "we don't know", not "nobody buys". That is why gate 1 trusts the strongest witness.

Amazon, measured twice a few hours apart

First runSecond run

10 of 12 stayed in the same strength band. Typical drift ±5–20%, worst +45% (lash serum). Amazon numbers are good to an order of magnitude, not to the unit.

AttackWhat we foundWhat we changed
Is Amazon search on target?No. "Hair growth serum" returned a Nutrafol pill; "men's incontinence underwear" returned Depend diapers.Count only listings whose title matches the product.
Is Meta phrase search clean?No. "Chin" and "collagen mask" ads came from med-spas and nail salons; "seat cushion" from car-parts shops.Meta only confirms; 13 noisy phrases ignored.
Does Meta phrase search see DTC brands?Poorly. Earthly Derm alone has 44 ads running 6+ months; the phrase "turmeric soap" finds 12.Where we measured the leader's domain earlier (16 products), use the larger number.
Is one TikTok page enough?No. 2 of 8 re-checks flipped from weak to strong.Weak TikTok = unknown, never a reason to drop.
Different keywords, different answer?Turmeric soap: 55K (bar) vs 119–131K ("dark spots", "kojic") a month. Men's leak-proof boxers: 2.5K vs 3.6K.Strong stayed strong, weak stayed weak.
