# Data audit before the investor dossier (2026-11-09)

Goal: find the large errors (not rounding). Each check says what was tested, how, what it found and what changes.
Tiers: ◆ confirmed by a platform or primary source · ◈ likely · ◇ disputed / single weak source.

## 1. "New brand" measured the wrong thing — the biggest error

**What we did before.** A brand counted as new if its oldest Amazon listing (dated from the product code, ASIN) was under 24 months old. That measures *time on Amazon in this product*, not the age of the business.

**Check.** For every brand counted as new with 1,000+ units/mo (71 brands, `new_brands.json`), we looked for its own site, domain registration, earliest web-archive copy, store review history and public company facts (`brand_age.py`, `brand_age.json`, web search).
The automatic part (domain + archive) **fails for brands with ordinary words as names** (luxe.com, copper.com, maree.com, tower.com were registered by other owners in the 1990s), so the decisive brands were checked by hand:

**What it means.**
- The three color-foundation "new brands" are 5-6-year-old direct-to-consumer (DTC) businesses that launched this product recently and brought it to Amazon in 2025-26. The diffuser's biggest "new" brand is 80 years old.
- So the old headline "66% of color foundation goes to brands under a year old" is **wrong**. The correct statement: "66% goes to brands that entered this product on Amazon in the last year; all three leaders are small DTC companies founded 2020-21 that built the product on Meta ads first." ◆
- Real new businesses (under ~2-3 years) that reached 1,000+/mo: Terra Lotus (tallow, TikTok-first), Kaori (persimmon, Meta-first), and many Amazon-only sellers with no site (RICHGRAND, ELBBUB, Treeroot, Magichome, PHOFAY, Graphene…), whose real age and origin are unknown (often established Chinese manufacturers selling under a new name) ◈.

**Fix.** Each brand gets two ages: *business age* (earliest of: store reviews, company facts, domain/archive when the name is distinctive) and *product age on Amazon* (ASIN). The dossier reports both and splits leaders by channel (Amazon / Meta / TikTok). "New brand" in text = business under 24 months, only where verified.

## 2. Amazon listings: is it really our product?

Read the listings covering 75-90% of units in all 16 script-scanned categories (`amz_spotcheck.py`).
- **Top 5: clean.** Color foundation, tallow, diffuser, persimmon: the listings are the product. ◆
- **Body oil is "all body oils".** Palmer's, Neutrogena, Aveeno etc. are general body oils, not oils sold for crepey skin. Our product's real niche (firming/crepey) is GODA + a few small sellers; the 196,800 shelf is the wider market. ◆
- **Compression socks: ~43,000 units/mo dropped** because those cards were saved with the brand name only (CHARMKING, FITRELL, FuelMeFoot…): the filter could not see the word "compression". Category is undercounted by about 40%. ◆ (does not change its "weak" status: these are old brands)
- Smaller leaks: a filter-replacement pack in pet fountains (4,000), an edge-control gel and scalp balms in rosemary oil, a face cream with "neck" in neck cream (7,000), two hair-removal creams excluded by a too-wide stop word ("Razorless", 20,000). None changes a verdict. ◆
- **Double counting of variants: not found.** Shades/sizes of one product share a review count but show their own "bought" counts (Luxe Light 80,000 vs Medium 70,000), so they are separate sales. ◈
- **The 4 hand-collected categories** (assistant, AI-assisted):
  - Lash serum: listings are the product. ◆
  - **Hair serum and pre-wash oil overlap with rosemary oil and with each other.** Hair serum's #1 is a rosemary water spray (Bare Anatomy, 70,000); pre-wash oil is really "all hair oils" (Moroccanoil, OGX, Mielle). Mielle, Lebanta, Bask, Botanic Hearth appear in two or three of these categories. They are one market (hair-growth oils and sprays) counted three times; pre-wash oil as defined is not a separate product. ◆
  - Whitening powder: the stop word "toothpaste" dropped PurelyWHITE (3,000/mo, a powder that says "toothpaste" in its title). Minor. ◆
  - The numbers themselves (badges, prices) can only be confirmed on Amazon: needs a person to re-open 10 random rows per category and compare. Task for the owner/assistant.

## 7. Rule change v2.2: no category is dropped on search alone (owner instruction, 2026-11-09)

Reason given by the owner: buyers may move part of their searching to AI assistants, and people change words, so Google alone can mislead. Checked:
- **Is Google search in this sector shrinking overall?** No. Five broad personal-care terms pulled together (shampoo ×1.08, body lotion ×1.32, moisturizer ×1.09, deodorant ×1.07, face wash ×1.24): their sum is ×1.13 a year. Our control correction (×1.12) matches it. ◆ An AI shift specific to one niche cannot be seen in this data; that is why the other signals now weigh the same.
- **Word changes.** All product phrasings are now pulled together in one comparison and summed. Tallow: tallow balm ×1.25, beef tallow for skin ×0.61, tallow skincare ×1.00, tallow moisturizer ×0.67 → **sum ×0.88, ×0.78 after the sector correction**: flat to falling, not a collapse. Turmeric: turmeric soap ×0.76, kojic acid soap ×0.83, turmeric bar ×1.02 → **sum ×0.81, ×0.72 corrected**: falling. ◆
- Note: this rule was changed after the results were seen, at the owner's instruction; v2.1 (search < ×0.8 drops a category) is kept for comparison.

**v2.2 decision rule.** Four signals, each scored −1 / 0 / +1, read together:
1. Search, all product phrasings summed, sector-corrected (≥1.2 → +1, 0.8-1.2 → 0, <0.8 → −1).
2. Leaders' own-store reviews, Aug-Oct vs a year ago and the last 3 months' direction.
3. Leaders' Meta ads by store domain: many ads alive 90+ days → +1; few → 0.
4. A verified young or small business reached a large share recently (business age, not Amazon age).
A category is dropped only when at least two signals are −1.
